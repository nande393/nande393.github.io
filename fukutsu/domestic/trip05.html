Tokyo

I took part in Peru tour with my wife and departed from Haneda Airport. So we stopped at Tokyo for going and returning. For such a reason, We stayed at Tokyo for extra 3 days. When I was a businessman, I visited Tokyo about one a year but the areas are only business zones, Akasakamituke, Shinjiku, Shinbushi, Nihonbasi etc. This time we strolled about towns that were called Shitamachi where the people in Edo period lived.

Tokyo "Shitamachi" stroll 01

Shibamata Taisyakuten 01Shibamata Taisyakuten 02YakatabuneTsukisima

Shibamata where hero Torasan in the popular movie was brought up is famous for shitamati. We walked all the day, looking around town to town. Tokyo was the capital of the water and some river through this big city. Then we found ships which move up and down for sightseening called yakatabune. Tsukishima is reclaimed land made in the Meiji era. Monjya street in this town is famous for Monjayaki which is a type of Japanese pan-fried batter.

Tokyo, especially shitamati interests us and we feel towns nostalgic because we know most of these town names in period drama and films. We strolled about Tsukudazima, Fukagawa and these areas where outlaws hung around in Edo period. Before World War II, Kagurazaka was a prospered district as the world of the geisha. I didn't know the name, Kappabashi before a few year. This town is popular not only with customers but also with foreigners for goods which restaurants use.
