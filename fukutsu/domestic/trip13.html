Tohoku

I and my wife took part in "Entirely Tohoku for four days in spring" tour.
I visited Tohoku for the first time that consisted of 6 prefectures, Aomori, Akita, Iwate, Yamagata, Miyagi and Fukushima, locate in the north part of Japan.

Miharu in Fukushima prefecture   4/22

Waterfall CherryWaterfall CherryWaterfall Cherry full bloom image

This tree is called "Waterfall Cherry" and is more than one thousand years old. "It is classified as one of the five great cherry trees of Japan and one of the three giant cherry trees of Japan." It was not until I visited Miharu that I realized what that cherry was. The full bloom had already done 1 week ago and I disappointed at it that almost lost blossoms looked pity. This tree is so very old that it seem powerless and can't keep bloom for long time.
Normally ume, peach and cherry bloom gradually in order, but around here, the north part of Japan, they bloom altogether when spring come. By the way "Miharu" means "three spring" in Japanese.
The picture, "full bloom image", was taken on 15th of April in this year.

Tyuson-ji in Iwate prefecture   4/23

Golden HallGolden HallGolden Hallinside the temple

This temple which locates in Hiraizumi-tyo is well known as the gold culture of Fujiwara three generation. Especially, the Golden Hall decorated with gold is famous and the hall is covered with a house to prevent from weather. Actually for tourists, photography are not allowed to Golden Hall, so two pictures of four are from the internet.

Kakunodate in Akita prefecture   4/23

Samurai Houses StreetSamurai Houses Street>Samurai Houses StreetSamurai Houses Street


Kakunodate is a castle town and it remain old days of Edo era with many samurai houses. There are many weeping cherry and some Yoshino cherry in this street. It was full bloom when we called on, and we enjoyed the cherry blossom viewing.

Hinokinai-gawa Bank   4/23

row of cherry treesrow of cherry treestunnel of cherry treestunnel of cherry trees

Hinokinai-gawa is a river that flows by Kakunodate samurai houses street, and is famous for a beauty spot of cherry. Most cherry are Yoshino cherry, and they line on the both banks. It was fine on that day and Yoshino Cherry was full bloom. Matching transparent blue water of the river and full blossoms, were picturesque.

Hirosaki in Aomori prefecture   4/24

There are a gigantic number of cherry trees in this Hirosaki Park. When we visited this park, cherry blossoms were at their best. The moat of the castle and the castle suited gorgeous cherry blossoms. And also far-off Mount Iwaki covered with snow harmonized with cherry blossoms in full bloom. Walking through the castle park and looking at a lot of blossoms, that was breathtaking scenery.

Matu-shima in Miyagi prefecture   4/25

islandsislandsislandsislands

Matsu-shima is the name of total islands that consist of more than 260 small or big islands surrounding forth and back Matsu-shima gulf. It is one of the Three Sights of Japan. However I disappointed a little bit, because I have seen the same sort of view at Kujuku-shima, 208 islands, in Nagasaki where I lived before.

This time I enjoyed many beautiful and fantastic cherry blossoms in Tohoku area and special sightseeing spots, Tyuson-ji and Matsu-shima.
