Nikko-Toshogu / Zenkouji / Tokyo

I hoped to go to Toshogu and Zenkoji for long time. Because I have remembered nothing about Toshogu and have never been to Zenkoji. This time I added Tokyo Skytree and Kawagoe to them, and planned a 5 nights 6 days trip for these tourist spots with my wife. Furthermore I searched some foreign country dishes in only Tokyo.

Kinugawa Onsen [Day 1]

Kinugawa Onsen town view
At 6:30 we left home for Kinugawa Onsen. Our flight bound for Haneda was at 9:00. I worried if I could find each station to get on and could change the line smoothly and could get to final station on time smoothly. Anyway we took a way forward. As soon as we arrived at Haneda we took Keikyu line to Asakusa. Then we changed the route to Tobu railway which took us to Kinugawa Onsen. At 14:30 we reached the station and walked to the Hotel. Everything went well in spite of my anxiety. Before dinner, we strolled around the view spots nearby, which were the rope bridge, the observatory on a cliff and the small waterfall, along the the river and the deep gorge. They are beautiful but not special. Then we took hot spring bath and had dinner which was viking style and was drink free. We enjoyed the onsen and eating.

Nikko Tosyogu Shrine [Day 2]

Shinkyu (Sacred Stable)Yomeimon GateKaramon Gate (Chinese Gate)Nemurineko (Sleeping cat)

Unfortunately, it was raining that day. We joined "Nikko Regular Sightseeing Bus Tour" at Kinugawa-onsen station. Following the tour, we visited Kegonnotaki waterfall, Futarasan Shrine and Tyuzenji temple, then we had lunch at Kanaya Hotel which is famous for royal family or well-known people coming. After lunch we stopped in Rinoji temple. Finally, we arrived at Toshogu. We passed Torii and the first gate to find some storehouses, the stable which is famous for the three monkeys, and a five storied pagoda. I was surprised and excited at them, because they were so beautiful, solemn and colorful enough. Then we walk forward and got to Yomeimon which has complicated curves and the structure. What a fine gate it was! Two years before, it was renewed so that it looked the new gate, especially not only carvings are delicate and beautiful, but also color and coating is fantastic. Furthermore, we looked in Karamon, the sleeping cat on the other gate, and the painting on the ceiling called Dragon Barks which makes echo like a little bell.
At 16:23 we headed for Asakusa from Tobu-Nikko station. It left a impression on me that around this area we sometimes could see beautiful full bloom of cherry blossom which was called Yamazakura in Japanese.

"Yomeimon Gate : Designated a National Treasure One of the most beautiful gates in Japan, Yomeimon Gate is said to have been given the name "Main Gate of the Imperial Court." It is also called "Gate of the Setting Sun" because one could gaze upon it all day and never tire. It is covered with over 500 carvings depicting traditional anecdotes, children playing, sages and wise men."

KabukizaTotyoa view from Totyo 45FMain Olinpic Stagiam

In the morning, it was raining so hard that we stopped going to Tokyo Skytree we planned. At 9:30 we left for Kabukiza by subway. In front of it I took photos of it but I couldn't enter it. We went to the basement floor where we could find a lot of Kabuki goods and food. Then we headed for Shinjuku Station to get Shinkansen tickets I have reserved before coming. When we arrived at Totyo, it had stopped raining. The nearer we look up, the bigger and taller Totyo was. At noon we had lunch at the staff dining hall of Totyo. I was surprise at many people in the hall. And food was reasonable price. After lunch we went up 45F south wing observatory, 202 meter high. Shinjuku has many skyscrapers so as to command good view like New York. Then we went to New National Stadium to know how big it was. Although it was under construction, we could see it's real scale. It was big enough to be amazed. Next we left for Roppongi to see Roppongi hills and Mori garden until dinner. However, we were embarrassed with too much time, because the garden was too small to kill time and we were tired in order to walk other places anymore. We took a rest around the garden and went to the restaurant early. It was the first time to have Sweden cuisine. The appetizer of pickling herring and salmon was very delicious.

Zenkouji Temple [Day 4]

Nio GateThe main TempleThe Front ApproachTokyo Station

Arriving at Nagano Station at 9:00, we had a volunteer guide for Omotesandou, the approach to the main temple, tour. In front of the Zenkoui is not a way but a kind of town. There are a lot of shops, temples and people paying a visit. We had explanation mainly about the shrines and temples. Zenkoji is a big and beautiful temple. We joined O-KAIDAN tour at the basement of temple where we just walked in complete darkness for about 10 minutes in order to know something in life. Then we strolled the temple garden. And we entered Kyogura which was a storehouse has a large round bookshelf for the scripture of Buddhism. It is said, "If only you make a rotation of it, it follows that you have read all of them once already. On the way to return I ate Zyuwari-Soba noodle, Oyakia dumpling and Sake Manzyu bun far too much so as to feel something wrong with the stomach. We left Ueno for Nagano early in the morning by Shinkansen, but for the return we arrived at Tokyo station early in the evening to see the renewed old style station. It was fantastic and beautiful. Many tall buildings around Tokyo station reminded me of New York Wall Street. For the supper we had nothing but Sweden dessert we took out, because we were full.

Kawagoe [Day 5]

Taisho-roman StreetKura no machi StreetKashiya YokochoKawagoe Hikawa Shine

We ate Oyaki for breakfast at our room in Hotel. I like Oyaki so much. Firstly, we visited Sensoji temple in Asakusa we had stayed at. Then we headed for Kawagoe, using subway and Seibu railway. I had decided where to go and what food to eat in Kawagoe before coming. We visited Kitain temple, Taisho-roman Street, Kura no machi Street, traditional Time Bell Tower, Kashiya Yokocho street and Kawagoe Hikawa Shrine. I was wondered at Kawagoe having so many temples, although I don't know why. On the way of our visiting we stopped in some temples. Kawagoe was nostalgic enough as a Edo era town. Then we had lunch at Ogawakiku, the most famous eel restaurant there. Eel Kabayaki was very delicious as rumored. Strolling the town all day long, we enjoyed a lot but I was so exhausted.

Tokyo 2 [Day 6]

Tokyo Skytreea view from Skytreea view from SkytreeShinjuku-Gyoen Garden

It was a beautiful sunny day. We went to Tokyo Skytree through Sumida park, one of famous viewpoints, on foot, looking up at it and taking photos of it. Some people had lined in spite of early time when we got there at before 8:00 as the first customer. The high speed elevator took us to the observatory, 350 meter, soon. The tower commanded a splendid view widely. However, the tower is so tall that all building looked tiny although few tall building like those of Shinjuku are there around it. Then we took a rest until Soramach arcade opened at 10:00 and we looked around shops of Soramachi. After that we headed for Shinjuku for lunch. We had made reservation for french lunch before. That restaurant is famous and reasonable but tasted so so. After lunch we walked about 5 minutes to Shinjuku-Gyoen garden. We stay there no more than one hour, strolling the garden. Though the garden has large lawn area, tree area and Japanese garden area, we couldn't be excited so much. Maybe it is a good place for local people to take it easy or to relax. Then we headed for Haneda early. We took the flight bound for Fukuoka at 19:00.

We changed some parts of this trip because of the weather and our physical condition. I really felt my aged this time.
For this trip I prepared traffic IC cards. That was why we had to use many means of transportation. As a result IC card was very convenience. I didn't need to look for a ticket counter or machine and we could use any vehicle by it.
During the trip we visited so many temples, that were big and small. We ate popular restaurant food and local food.
We enjoyed our trip a lot in spite of small troubles.
