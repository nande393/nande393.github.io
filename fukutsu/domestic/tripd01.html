Sakitsu Village in Amakusa

Sakitsu Village is the part of the World Cultural Heritage as gHidden Christian Sites in the Nagasaki Regionh. First we visited there because it seemed to be worth seeing a historical and mysterious area.
The Edo govern-ment issued the ban on Christianity and expelled the missionaries. Hidden Chris-tians who kept their faith in secret and lived in society with Japanese traditional religions for 250 years.

The Meiji govern-ment dismissed the prohibition on Christianity and those people come back to Catholic. They build Catholic church. That time in his area 500 families were Christians among 600 families. Sakitsu Church was build in 1934 as Gothic style. The old Sakitsu Church next to Sakitsu Suwa Shrine was build in 1885.

In Imatomi village close neighbor Sakitsu, a unique form of worshipping developed here as Christian rituals combined with those of Bud-dhism and Shugendo asceticism. One of symbol was Umantera-sama.

Maria Statue Over the Ocean was built in 1974 was the symbol not only of faith to God but also of pray for the security of the fishing of the fisherman.
