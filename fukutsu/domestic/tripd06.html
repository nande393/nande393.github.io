Hokkaido (Sapporo, Furano-Biei)


I had visited Hokkaido twice in winter, but never in summer. While we enjoyed snow festivals and other winter activities, the natural landscapes were completely covered in snow during those trips. This time, I wanted to explore Hokkaido in summer, often showcased on TV for its vast landscapes, lush vegetable fields, and stunning flower fields, particularly the iconic lavender fields. Furano, located in the heart of Hokkaido, is renowned for these lavender fields. Although Ifm not a big fan of alcohol, the Sapporo Beer Garden is famous for its local beer and Mongolian mutton barbecue, so I planned to visit both Sapporo and Furano with my wife.

First day (Sapporo)

Sapporo Beer GardenFormer Hokkaido Goverment Office BuildingSapporo Clock TowerSapporo TV Tower

We left Fukuoka for New Chitose Airport at 8:00 AM and took a train to Sapporo Station. Unfortunately, I missed the bus to the Sapporo Beer Garden, where we had a reservation, so we took a taxi. There, we enjoyed Mongolian mutton barbecue paired with beer and visited the small museum in the garden. We then walked to the Former Hokkaido Government Office Building and Sapporo Clock Tower. At Odori Park and Sapporo TV Tower, we relaxed, ate grilled corn and baked potatoes with butter as an early supper, and headed to our hotel.

Second day

Part 1 (Furano)

Farm TomitaFarm Tomitafield viewJet Coaster Road

Our schedule was tight as we needed to catch the "Biei Panorama Course" sightseeing bus in Furano. At 6:00 AM, we enjoyed a delicious seafood bowl at the hotel buffet, featuring scallops and crab. We hurried to Furano Station and boarded the bus. Our first stop, Farm Tomita, disappointed us as the lavender had lost its color due to Junefs abnormal weather. However, the bus ride through Furanofs beautiful scenery, including vast green landscapes and the 4km-long Jet Coaster Road, was unforgettable.

Part 2 (Biei)

Ken & Mary TreeHokusei no Oka View ParkShikisai no OkaShikisai no Oka


In Biei, the Ken & Mary Tree, named after a 1972 Nissan Skyline commercial, and the view from Hokusei no Oka View Park showcased the areafs stunning natural beauty. Shikisai no Oka was a highlight, with its vibrant flower fields, especially the lavender, which made up for our disappointment at Farm Tomita.

Part 3 (Biei)

Blue PondShirahige FallsNorokko Train No. 8soup curry restaurant

What a beautiful Blue Pond! It was said to have formed naturally, with trees growing on what is now the pond floor before it filled with water, and the striking blue color comes from aluminum components carried by the river. Shirahige Falls, a 30-meter cascade, resembles a white beard, adding to the areafs charm. Bieifs rich natural beauty truly captivated us. We got off the sightseeing bus at JR Biei Station and headed to Sapporo, riding the Norokko sightseeing train from Biei to Asahikawa along the way. Back in Sapporo, we ordered takeout soup curry, a specialty of Hokkaido, from the famous Rojiura Curry Samurai near Sapporo Station. We arrived at our hotel at 8:20 PM and ate the soup curry, which surprised me with its incredible flavor?I hadnft expected it to be so delicious.

Third day (Sapporo)

Okurayama Ski Jump Stadiumfrom the observation deckShiroi Koibito ParkNijo Fish Market

We joined the "Morning Sightseeing Bus" tour from 9:00 AM to around 2:00 PM, visiting Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium, Shiroi Koibito Park, and Nijo Fish Market. Okurayama Ski Jump Stadium is often featured on TV during ski jump competitions. Standing at the top of the jump platform, I felt intimidated by its incredible height. Shiroi Koibito Park is not just a factory but a theme park that seems to delight children. We enjoyed lunch at Nijo Fish Market, savoring fresh seafood. Although we had some time to spare, we left Sapporo for New Chitose Airport to spend the remaining time there.

Afterword
Before the trip, I thought two nights and three days would be enough, as our main goal was to see the lavender fields at Farm Tomita and experience Hokkaidofs vast summer landscapes. As a result, we marveled at the natural beauty and felt fulfilled, but I also realized we missed many places and left much to explore. Even so, we were thoroughly satisfied and truly enjoyed this trip.
