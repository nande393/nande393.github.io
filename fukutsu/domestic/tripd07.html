Nagasaki


I lived in Nagasaki for about 10 years about 34 years ago, when I worked for a company as a businessman. After retiring, I had only visited twice with a friend to eat Chinese food at Kouzanrou in Urakami. However, I still have many fond memories of the places and buildings in Nagasaki.

First day

Part 1

Nagasaki StationNagasaki Shinchi ChinatownThe Former Chinese SettlementDejima

I arrived at Nagasaki Station and was surprised to see the surroundings. The station that I knew was somewhat dark and quite old. Now, I saw that it had become spacious and modern.
Before long, we headed to Chinatown. Then we strolled there, and the streets brought back memories. We ate "sweet-and-sour pork" and "Chinese fried rice" and found them delicious at Shinwarou, which I used to visit sometimes when I lived in Nagasaki.
This was the first time I had ever been to "The Former Chinese Settlement," although I lived there for many years. It seems to have been recently renovated, creating a special atmosphere.
We did not know about Dejima, which was rebuilt about 10 years ago. I only knew the miniature of that town exhibited there. Those houses were fine, but it didn't attract me much because of my lack of interest.

Part 2

streetcar(trame) Megane(glasses) Bridge Hamano-machi Arcade

In Nagasaki, we used trams to move everywhere. Trams have provided both important transportation and a special atmosphere to Nagasaki through the ages.
We headed to the hotel to check in and take a rest. After that, we left the hotel for the restaurant where "Turkish rice" was originally created. On the way, we stopped by Megane (Spectacles) Bridge and Hamano-machi Arcade, thinking of the old days. I had high expectations for the restaurant, but its taste was terrible, which disappointed me greatly.

Second day

a view of prefectural goverment observation deck sloped residential areas Nagasaki Stadium City Nagasaki's Confucious Mausoleum

The next day, we first headed for the prefectural government observation deck to see the nice scenery. The sea we saw was beautiful, and the Mitsubishi docks were still standing in the view.
Next, we strolled to Nagasaki Stadium City. This course was comfortable because greenery was lined along the clean streets. On the way, I found sloped residential areas and thought, "This is the unique atmosphere of Nagasaki." We arrived at Nagasaki Stadium City. It looked new and seemed to be widely accepted by people for soccer games, shopping, and restaurants. To have lunch, we went to Chinatown again, and at Kouzanrou, which I like the most, we ate "Dongpo pork" and "Sara Udon using thick noodles with various toppings." Of course, it was very delicious.
Then we went to "Nagasaki Confucius Mausoleum" and enjoyed looking around after a long time. We were about to leave when it started raining, so we stopped sightseeing and headed to the station to wait for the Shinkansen. We walked around the shops in the station to kill time while waiting, but it was boring. The Shinkansen runs from Takeo-Onsen to Nagasaki as the Nishi-Kyushu line. At the transfer station, two trains shared the same platform and faced each other, so the connection was smooth.

Afterword

We had a good time on this trip, especially bringing back the old days. The more time I spent there, the more I remembered the town where I used to work and various old memories.

However, I made a serious mistake on this trip. On the train to Hakata, I checked the tickets on my mobile phone and found that the site had already logged me out. I definitely forgot to bring my ID and password, so I was in a panic. As soon as I arrived at Hakata station, I caught a station staff member and asked him to help us. I was still in a panic, so I couldn't do anything and didn't know what to do. The station staff member kindly asked me some keywords to solve the problem. Finally, he gave me a hint to look for the email message sent from JR before. After some difficulty, I succeeded in opening the site and finding the tickets. But we couldn't catch the Shinkansen we had reserved because time ran out. These tickets cannot be used for other trains officially, but the station staff member was kind enough to change them specially for us. So, the tickets got us on the next Shinkansen one hour later.

My failure was pitiful and terrible, but it might become one of the memorable moments of the trip in the future. People were so warm and kind, especially the station staff, that we had a wonderful time and enjoyed the trip. Thanks a lot.
