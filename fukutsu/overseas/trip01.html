Taipei

city centerThe Presidential Office BuildingThe National Palace MuseumChiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall

The Presidential Office Building originally served as the seat of the colonial government during the Japanese occupation period.
The National Palace Museum is one of best four in the world. When The Republic of China government moved to Taiwan, as you know a lot of historic treasures of China were brought here.
The National Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall is a famous monument erected in memory of Chiang Kai-shek, former President of the Republic of China.
