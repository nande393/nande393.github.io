Peru

I went to Peru in the early spring last year with my wife, joining in a Ten days Peru tour. For this presentation, I picked up 16 photographs that we took in Peru. And I'll simply introduce you four places.

Cusco

Cusco 01Cusco 02Cusco 03Cusco 04

Cusco is located in the Andes at an elevation of 3600 meters. Cusco was the capital of Inca Empire which was built between eleventh and twelveth century.

Cusco / Lake Titicaca

Cusco 05Cusco 06Lake Titicaca 01Lake Titicaca 02

Lake Titicaca is 3810 meters above the sea and is the highest navigable lake in the world. Native people still live on some small floating islands which are made of reels.

Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu01 Machu Picchu 02Machu Picchu 03Machu Picchu 04

Machu Picchu is a popular Inca ruin and is located on a high mountain ridge. It's altitude is about 2350 meters.

Lines and Geoglyphs of Nazca

Nazca 01Nazca 02Nazca 03Nazca 04

Lines and Geoglyphs of Nazca is a famous world heritage as you know. There are about 700 drawings including various birds, animals and so on. Most of them were created during the Nazca culture between 200 BC and 500 AD. Surprisingly, the lines were more narrow and shallow than I have thought.
