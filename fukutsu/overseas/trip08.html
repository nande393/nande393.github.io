Taiwan

This was the third time to go to Taiwan. My wife and I took part in the tour, too. The last time we enjoyed such Chinese dishes in Taiwan that we decided to go again. We visited many famous tourist spots before, so we didn't want to see them a lot. However we might not enjoy Chinese dishes without doing nothing, staying at hotels. This time we visited near places of Taipei which had good atmosphere as rural sites of Taiwan. We did not expect sightseeing but the places we visited delighted us a lot.

Shi Fen & Jiu Fen

Shi FenJiu Fen Shi Fen Jiu Fen Jiu Fen

Shi FenJiu Fen means ten families and Jiu Fen is for nine families. Both villages were seemed to be small and rural site.
I saw lanterns which was made of paper flying in the sky. After about ten minutes they fall down mountains. That was very fantastic and illusion. At first I doubted if it was dangerous, because they fly by air burnt with kind of papers attached. I knew the fact around here is very dump so it is difficult for lanterns to burn in mountains.

There are a unique shopping arcade and restaurants in Jiu Fen. They are very nostalgic for us because they looked like a mixture of Chinese and old Japanese style. I heard one of buildings was used as the model of the animation movie, "Sen and Chihiro".
