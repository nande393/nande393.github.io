Two noted mountains in Switzerland, the Romantic Road, Mount-Saint-Michel, Paris and London for eleven days

I visited four countries in Europe with my wife, taking part in a eleven days tour from Fukuoka. At first we planned a trip in Europe as a private excursion for a month but we couldn't have confidence to do it. However eleven days were too short to visit four countries. We visited and saw a lot of places and took more than 1,500 pictures. Our tour was so hurried that we couldn't understand what we saw and sometimes we were embarrassed, although we enjoyed a bit long tour.

This small town just looked a town for tourists. Because we couldn't see any houses, but shops, wine gardens and restaurants. We entered a Japanese owner's wine seller and enjoyed wine testing of various kind of wine.

Then we walked to a landing place for the Rhine cruise. We went down the river toward the north from Rudesheim near Frankfurt to St.Goar for about 1 hour and 40 minutes, looking over wine fields, towns and old castles along the river.

Jungfrau [5]

Jungfraujochglaciermountains and a trainmountains and us

After enjoying the castle, we headed toward Interlaken in Switzerland which is an important and well-known tourist destination because of the main transport gateway to the Swiss Alps, the mountains and lakes. It has a population of less 6,000 as a small but prospered fine town. We had a dinner with a show of folk and walked to our hotel at about 11 o'clock, but it was not night as if it was daytime.
Next day we took our bus to the station of mountain railway for Jungfraujoch. Then we got on a mountain climbing train and on the way of Jungfraujoch, views from a train delighted us, showing mountains covered with snow and huge green fields skirting mountains with small alpine flowers. We reached the final station, Jungfraujoch. We stepped to the observatory soon and faced a great view that the top of high mountains was too close to us. It absolutely made us upset.
"The Jungfraujoch is an Alpine attraction and viewing point, at an altitude of 3,454 m. It is located in between the well known mountains Monch and Jungfrau, in the heart of the UNESCO Natural World Heritage property, "Jungfrau - Aletsch - Bietschhorn". The cogwheel train is the only way to get there. The train station is the highest one in Europe."

"The Matterhorn is famous for that reason, The most obvious is its shape, especially in the classic view from Zermatt. A tall rocky fang with a summit that appears to overhang the sheer north face, the first view of the peak is breathtaking."
We left Zermatt for Gornergrat to see The Matterhorn by a mountain climbing train. Next, we stood at Gornergrat observatory where 4,000m class mountains was surrounding us. The place, 3,131m, gets near the top of mountains and it give us actually impact as magnificent and strongly impressed.
Then we downed the mountain by train and on the way we hiked from Rotenboden to Riffelberg for about 1 hour and half, 2.2Km long and vertical drop 232m. Looking around mountains, green land and alpine plant, we enjoyed the hike, furthermore good weather made us more fun. However the hilight of the hiking a lot was an inverted image of Mt.Matterhorn on Riffelsee lake. Fortunately we could see it and were excited for the beauty.
