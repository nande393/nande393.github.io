Dambulla cave temple

the exterior viewinside Caveinside CaveThe Golden Buddha statue

"Dambulla cave temple is also known as the Golden Temple of Dambulla is a World Heritage Site, situated in the central part of the country. "
"Dating back to the First Century BC, the Golden Temple of Dambulla has been the centre of pilgrimage for Buddhists and Hindus alike for 22 centuries. It is Sri Lanka's most popular historic site. The Cave monastery, home to Buddhist monks is covered with exquisite 2,000 year-old murals depicting the life and times of the Lord Buddha. The shrines also house a collection of 157 statues of Buddha in various sizes and poses, including a 15 metre long reclining Buddha and vividly coloured frescoes on the walls and ceiling, making this the largest antique painted surface in the world."
"The Golden Buddha statue, Dambulla golden temple, just downhill from the Cave temple."

Sri Lanka is a Buddhist country, 70% of the people are Buddhist who have long history and culture and the great heritage.
It looked a big stone mountain and has 5 caves as temples. Each cave was made by different era for enormous long time, which seemed belief profoundly.

Sigiria Rock

the exterior viewFrescogiant stone lion's pawsthe staircase

Built by an obsessed monarch in the 5th century. It rises a sheer 200 metres above a forested plain, altitude of 370 meters. On the way to the top, one third, there is the small ground with the remains of a pair of giant stone lion's paws still guarding the staircase that leads to the summit, once occupied by a royal palace, 180 m long and 100m long.

Entire 1,200 steps, 1,000 steps from the open space, were hard work for us. Queer and interesting palace, like the king's life who killed his father, had gardens, a reservoir and even 500 women as a harem and many frescoes described beautiful seminude ladies, one of them are called Sigiria Lady. Now, most of the frescoes were erased by monks who lived in the place.
At the entrance I looked up at the rock and it is too steep, almost vertical, so I had no confidence to climb up the top of rock. However, the top was a amusing place where ruins of palace made us image of past days, and we could looked out over beautiful forests surrounding the top.

Galle Fort

the exteriorthe exteriorthe insidethe inside

"Dutch Fort at Galle, close to the island's southernmost point, 173km from Colombo, has the distinction of being the best-preserved sea fort in South Asia. A living heritage site, this 90 hectare attraction is a superb blend of architecture, with fortifications that resemble those in the coastal areas of Portugal. The fall of Galle to the Dutch in 1640 saw its fortifications consolidated further along the lines of the fortified cities of Europe. The Dutch and the English colonial styles are evident in the deep verandahs of houses supported by timber or masonry pillars. Originally established by the Portuguese in the 16th Century ..."

Fort was big enough like a town. We had lunch at a restaurant inside Fort. After lunch we strolled the inside, looking around many shops and a few old houses.
