Siem Reap

We arrived Siem Reap Airport is smaller than Phnom Penh. I had a glance but there was not taxi but some motor bicycle taxi. However, before coming I applied for a tour which included taking us to a hotel. Then I understood why the tour included our meeting at the airport.
One of enjoyable things in travel is eating local food. I had a list of restaurants which were searched by internet before coming, but I was so tired to found out one of them. So I heard some information from the hotel and went around there. After a while walking, fortunately I encountered one of restaurants in my list which used traditional house of Cambodia, made of wood and had very high floor.
Next day evening I ate Cambodia food again with watching traditional dance show. I tasted it nice. I felt Cambodia culture is very similar to Thailand because I had seen mostly same dance in Thailand as traditional dance. I heard that Siem Peap mean "lost" as Thailand was defeated by Cambodia, and both Cambodia and Thailand were integrated in the Angkor era.
Cambodia is poor. Some didn't ware shoes and some children didn't ware shirt. Most road are dusty and nasty, but only main road are paved. There are not taxi and city buses on streets so it is common that people used motor bicycle taxi. Now 80% people are still occupied in agriculture.

Cambodian has the tragedy in their history. All countries have it but it was special that the Pol Pot killed a lot of same country people the amount of whom were about half that age. Tuol Sleng is one of place where has the evidence of genocide as a prison. I saw a panel including many photos lined in two on and under, above lined the young in photos was killed and under lined the young in photos wearing hats killed them.
Now Cambodia steps forward development although tourists are not so many. However Cambodia still has many problems and education is one of them. There are not enough school rooms so students are divided two, morning class and afternoon class, to study. In addition seemingly teachers are sometimes absent or do part time job because their salary are very low. Really I met a group of students who said so in the morning at a museum garden. I heard that by the same reason public servant of Cambodia worked only in the morning.

Cambodia hotels cost higher than Malaysia. In Cambodia tourism is one of important industry and lives of tourists likely have distance from normal Cambodian. So tourists were likely to pay high cost to sightseeing a little bit.
I didn't see traffic jam in Cambodia and cars were not many. I heard driver license was need to drive but loose, and they seemed not to have many traffic rules therefore I saw tree, four or even five people rode on a motor bicycle together without helmets, it looked very dangerous.
Finally I bid to prefer Cambodia. People are so friendly and kind with smile.
