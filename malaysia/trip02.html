Vietnam

Mekong cruise

in front of Sinh CoffeMekong Delta 01Mekong Delta 02Mekong Delta 03

Seeing Mekong Delta was one of expectations of this excursion so we took part in Mekong Delta 2 days tour as a Shin Coffe tour which was very cheep, 18$ a person. We boated for a long time and sometime saw huge paddy fields over banks. At lunch time we met lovely Vietnamese children who maybe came there as a school trip were friendly and simple.

Meong Delta 04Meong Delta 05Meong Delta 06Meong Delta 07

Mekong Delta which is fertile grow rice and feed many people. People live there with Mekong river using the water of it to wash hair and clothes and even drink. So they seemed to depend on Mekong river. Although Mekong river is not so clean and makes small worse. Yet people seemed vivid and friendly so children and even adult shock hands to us cruising on it.

Mekong Delta 08Mekong Delta 09Cai Rang Floating Market 01Cai Rang Floating Market 02

Creeks like small and narrow waterways which are put around in Jangle were used to move freely as if they were streets during The Vietnam War. And there were many sheds along the creeks which were also used by soldiers to stay. One of them which looked bigger than others was a meeting hall. I have seen a floating market in Thailand which was not need real lifves for people living there so it was mainly for tourist. However here in Vietnam floating markets were of use to be alive and they were very real and vivid.
